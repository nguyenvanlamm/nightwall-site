# Nightwall: Climb unseen.

A one-tap stealth ninja climb for iPhone. **Coming soon to the App Store.**

Leap from wall to wall up a gorge at night. Stay out of the lantern light, and see how high you get before the eye opens.

**No ads · No tracking · Plays offline**

## The idea: two walls, one thumb, and the light

You remember the wall-jump ninja games on your first phones: one thumb, two walls, one more try. Nightwall keeps that rhythm and adds something you have to read: light. A spike on your wall is a reason to jump. A lantern swinging your way is a reason to wait.

- **Light is the enemy.** Lanterns swing across the gorge and light one wall at a time; a searchlight sweeps from the watchtower. Stay lit too long and the eye opens: you are spotted.
- **Gates you keep.** Every 300 m a stone ledge marks a gate. Fall at 1,140 m and you start again from gate 3, not from the ground. A stretch between gates takes under a minute.
- **One smoke bomb can save a climb.** A smoke bomb hides you for 2.5 seconds. Caught anyway? Spend one to vanish on the spot, once per climb. Each new gate earns one, up to 9.

## Screens: the gorge at night

- Home: start from any gate you have reached.
- Climbing: jump to the dark wall.
- Spotted: vanish, or retry from your last gate.

## How to play: learn it in one climb

1. **Tap anywhere.** The ninja leaps to the other wall.
2. **Watch the light.** Lanterns light one wall at a time. Jump to the dark one.
3. **Reach the gate.** Every 300 m you keep your progress. The Daily Climb is one tower for everyone each day.

## Questions

- **Does it have ads?** No. There are no ads anywhere in Nightwall, and no ad-based rewards.
- **Do you collect data?** No. Your progress is stored only on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Purchases go through Apple; we never see your payment details. Read the [privacy policy](https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/nightwall-site/privacy.html.md).
- **What do purchases add?** The game is free to play. Full Tower, a one-time purchase, opens two more worlds: The Bell Pass and The Watchtowers. Smoke-bomb packs add 5 or 15 smoke bombs. You can restore purchases on a new device from the settings on the home screen.
- **Do I need an internet connection?** No. Nightwall plays fully offline. The App Store is only needed to make or restore a purchase.
