The idea

Two walls, one thumb, and the light

You remember the wall-jump ninja games on your first phones: one thumb, two walls, one more try. Nightwall keeps that rhythm and adds something you have to read: light. A spike on your wall is a reason to jump. A lantern swinging your way is a reason to wait.

Light is the enemy

Lanterns swing across the gorge and light one wall at a time; a searchlight sweeps from the watchtower. Stay lit too long and the eye opens: you are spotted.

Gates you keep

Every 300 m a stone ledge marks a gate. Fall at 1,140 m and you start again from gate 3, not from the ground. A stretch between gates takes under a minute.

One smoke bomb can save a climb

A smoke bomb hides you for 2.5 seconds. Caught anyway? Spend one to vanish on the spot, once per climb. Each new gate earns one, up to 9.

How to play

Learn it in one climb

  1. Tap anywhere.The ninja leaps to the other wall.
  2. Watch the light.Lanterns light one wall at a time. Jump to the dark one.
  3. Reach the gate.Every 300 m you keep your progress. The Daily Climb is one tower for everyone each day.

Questions

Before you climb

Does it have ads?

No. There are no ads anywhere in Nightwall, and no ad-based rewards.

Do you collect data?

No. Your progress is stored only on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Purchases go through Apple; we never see your payment details. Read the privacy policy.

What do purchases add?

The game is free to play. Full Tower, a one-time purchase, opens two more worlds: The Bell Pass and The Watchtowers. Smoke-bomb packs add 5 or 15 smoke bombs. You can restore purchases on a new device from the settings on the home screen.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Nightwall plays fully offline. The App Store is only needed to make or restore a purchase.
