# Privacy Policy

Nightwall · last updated 14 October 2026

Nightwall is a one-tap climbing game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your progress: your best height in each world, the gates you have reached, your Daily Climb bests, your smoke bombs and jade.
- Your settings: whether you have already seen the first-climb hint.
- Whether you have bought Full Tower, together with the store transaction identifiers of your purchases, kept so that a purchase is never granted twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
- This website uses no cookies, analytics or third-party fonts.

## Purchases

The Gorge, with its endless climb and the Daily Climb, is free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

## Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
