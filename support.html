How to play

Tap anywhere to leap to the other wall. Lanterns light one wall at a time and the watchtower searchlight sweeps across the gorge: the eye at the top fills while you are lit, and when it is full you are spotted. Spikes and falling tiles knock you off. Every 300 m a gate saves your progress, so you can start the next climb from there. A smoke bomb (button at the bottom right) hides you for 2.5 seconds; when you are caught, one smoke bomb lets you vanish and keep climbing, once per climb.

Purchases

Nightwall is free to play. Full Tower is a one-time purchase that opens The Bell Pass and The Watchtowers. Smoke-bomb packs add 5 or 15 smoke bombs; you can hold up to 99, so a pack that would go over that cannot be bought. Purchases are handled by the App Store; for refunds, use Apple’s “Report a Problem”. Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? On the home screen, tap the gear (Settings), then Restore Purchases.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
